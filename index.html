Honest about the limits

  • Turn-based, not instant. One sentence at a time, a few seconds from speech to translated speech (about four with live audio). The badge above shows the real measured average for this session.
  • The mouth moves with the sound, not with phonemes. It opens and closes with audio loudness. It is not phoneme-accurate lip-sync — that needs a GPU and runs slower than real time.
  • No key, no invented translation. Without a Gemini key, only the three demo sentences translate. Anything else says so plainly instead of guessing.
